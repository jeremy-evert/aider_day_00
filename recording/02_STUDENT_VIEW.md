# What students should see

Students should see, in order: the repository and clean status; the spec and
one-file prompt; the Aider command and local model name; a small diff; passing
compile/tests/diff-check; the restored playable program; the seeded report;
and a final understandable Git status.

Students should hear that a model response is a proposal, not proof. They
should also hear why the prepared payoff is labeled prepared and why future
failing tests stay out of the visible suite until their chamber is introduced.
