# Primary sources

All sources below were reviewed 2026-08-30. Contractual documents are
identified separately from explanatory documentation.

## Microsoft contractual sources

- [Gen AI Contractual Terms FAQ](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/faqs/123)
  (Microsoft licensing). Says the organization's licensing agreement plus
  applicable Product Terms, DPA, and SLAs govern Microsoft 365 Copilot and
  Copilot Chat; it also distinguishes separate Bing web-query terms.
- [Microsoft Product Terms — Microsoft 365](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/en-US/productoffering/Microsoft365/)
  (current Product Terms). Review Microsoft 365 Copilot, Office 365 Services,
  Universal License Terms, and Privacy & Security Terms; the applicable
  licensing program and customer agreement still matter.
- [Microsoft Products and Services DPA](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs/view/Microsoft-Products-and-Services-Data-Protection-Addendum-DPA?lang=1)
  (current licensing document and archive index). The page identified the
  current English edition as May 22, 2026 when reviewed. Use the live page for
  the current/archived downloadable edition rather than freezing a local copy.

## Microsoft product, privacy, and model sources

- [Copilot Chat FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/faq) — EDP,
  organizational coverage, access, and product distinctions.
- [Enterprise data protection](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/enterprise-data-protection)
  — EDP commitments for prompts and responses.
- [Data, Privacy, and Security for Microsoft 365 Copilot](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/deployoffice/privacy/microsoft-365-copilot)
  — no foundation-model training for prompts, responses, and Graph data;
  model and web-grounding nuances.
- [Copilot data protection and auditing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/microsoft-365/microsoft-365-copilot-architecture-data-protection-auditing)
  and [Purview retention](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/retention-policies-copilot)
  — audit, eDiscovery, retention, and deletion behavior.
- [OpenAI as a Microsoft Online Services subprocessor](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/openai-subprocessor)
  — OpenAI-operated models versus Microsoft-operated Azure OpenAI models;
  OpenAI was listed June 23, 2026 and available July 9, 2026, with stated
  exclusions. This is an enterprise Microsoft relationship, not consumer
  ChatGPT.
- [AI subprocessors overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/copilot-subprocessor-overview)
  — Microsoft oversight and the possibility of Anthropic and other providers.
- [Microsoft 365 Copilot release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/release-notes)
  — model-selector changes such as GPT-5.2 and Claude; availability depends
  on license, rollout, administrator controls, and tenant configuration.

## SWOSU institutional sources

- [Information and Cyber Security Policy](https://bulldog.swosu.edu/administrative-services/information-technology/cyber-security-policy.php)
  — applies to faculty, staff, students, and granted users; requires
  authorized access, policy/law/copyright/licensing/contract compliance, and
  warns that activity may be reviewed or monitored.
- [System Privacy Policy](https://bulldog.swosu.edu/publications/policies/information-technology/privacy-policy.php)
  — created October 2021; describes personal-data, third-party, automatic
  collection, and security practices, with explicit limitations.
- [SWOSU AI Guidelines and Policies](https://www.swosu.edu/ai-resources/guidelines.php)
  and [AI Resources for Students](https://www.swosu.edu/ai-resources/students.php)
  — course-specific permission, attribution, accuracy checking, academic
  integrity, intellectual-property, and non-public research cautions.
- [SWOSU ITS Policy & Security hub](https://bulldog.swosu.edu/administrative-services/information-technology/policy-security/)
  — links the cyber, cloud-computing, SSO, privacy, and related policies.
