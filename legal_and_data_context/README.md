# Institutional AI: whose system are you using?

This folder explains the public, documented environment around Microsoft
Copilot/Copilot Chat used with an institutional account. It is teaching
context, not legal advice and not a substitute for your course syllabus,
SWOSU policy, or the controlling agreements.

## The short version

When we walk through the wet cement at the company we work for, the company
still owns the cement. That is an analogy about environment, control, and
rights boundaries—not a universal rule that resolves every student or
employment ownership question.

Access is not ownership. Single sign-on tells a service who you are; it does
not turn Microsoft's service, SWOSU's account, organizational data, logs, or
every visible artifact into your private property.
