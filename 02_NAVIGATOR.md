# 02 — Use AI as the Navigator

If you do not know the next mechanical step, ask one bounded question.

Useful opening questions:

1. What do I open to run a file?
2. Where should I put my file?
3. How do I create a file?
4. How do I write a tiny Python program?
5. How do I run my Python program?

Use this troubleshooting pattern when something does not match:

```text
Here is exactly what I expected:
<EXPECTED>

Here is exactly what I observed:
<OBSERVED>

Do not give me five fixes. Give me the safest next observation to make.
```

Do not paste passwords, tokens, private/student data, or unrelated terminal history.
